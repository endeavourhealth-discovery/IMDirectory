## Clear Suggested

* Open IMDirectory
* Login
* Search for "Scoliosis deformity of spine (disorder)"
* Select "Scoliosis deformity of spine (disorder)" result
* Click logo to return to homepage

* Click "Clear suggestions" button
* Click dialog confirm
