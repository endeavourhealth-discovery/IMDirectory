# Footer Bar

This specification describes the footer bar functionality of IMDirectory.

* Open IMDirectory

## Privacy Policy
* Click footer link "Privacy policy"
* Title "Privacy policy" is displayed

## Cookie Policy
* Click footer link "Cookie policy"
* Title "Cookies" is displayed

## SNOMED Agreement
* Click footer link "Snomed agreement"
* Title "SNOMED" is displayed

## UPRN Agreement
* Click footer link "Uprn agreement"
* Title "UPRN" is displayed

## Cookie Settings
* Click cookie settings button
* Cookie settings drawer is displayed
